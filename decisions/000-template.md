# 000 — Template

`date: YYYY-MM-DD` · `status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNN`

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Constraints, deadlines, what I did not know yet.
If there was no real constraint, there was no real decision and this file should not exist.

## Options

### Option A — <name>

What it is, and the strongest honest case for it.

### Option B — <name>

Same. Written well enough that someone who prefers B would say "yes, that is my argument".

## Decision

What I chose, in one sentence, and the single reason that actually decided it — not the list
of reasons assembled afterwards.

## Consequences

- What this now makes easy
- What this now makes hard or expensive
- What I am deferring, and roughly when it comes due

## What would change my mind

The specific observation that would make me reverse this. A number, a failure, a new
requirement. "If it turns out to be slow" is not specific enough; "if p99 write latency goes
above 200ms at 50 concurrent clients" is.

## Reviewed

`date:` — did the consequence I predicted happen? Left blank until it is revisited.
